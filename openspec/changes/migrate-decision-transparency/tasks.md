## 2. The recorded decision surface — Convex (`packages/convex-centaur-state/`, mounted by `packages/convex-host/`)

- [ ] 2.1 Define the canonical computed-display-state record shape as one exported TypeScript type in `packages/convex-centaur-state/src/index.ts` — per candidate direction: the worst-case weighted score, the worst-case simulated world including its per-snake turn timestamps, and one entry per contributing heuristic carrying its display label, its normalised output in that world, and the portfolio weight in force when the score was composed — with absence encoded as key-absence, never zero and never carried over, no field whose value a consumer would have to resolve against configuration, and the heuristic set typed once per snapshot (one weight per heuristic, outputs varying by direction) rather than independently shaped per direction — the shape follows the producer's guarantee that every heuristic answers every candidate, so the type has no per-direction optionality to model (`decision-transparency/computed-display-state`, `#the-same-heuristics-under-every-direction`, `decision-transparency/extensible-state-slots`, `bot-framework/total-heuristic-coverage`)
- [ ] 2.2 Replace the `schema.ts` placeholder with the real Centaur-state schema entry for the per-snake display record (one row per game+snake, indexed for the team's live subscription), keeping the row's payload columns loose enough to hold whole simulated worlds while the shape of 2.1 is the authority on their contents; the row lifecycle around it is not this capability's work — seeding a game's rows with no pre-existing state at provisioning belongs to `game-lifecycle`, and retaining them past instance teardown for the life of the game record belongs to `replay-and-audit` (`decision-transparency/computed-display-state`, `decision-transparency/extensible-state-slots`)
- [ ] 2.3 Implement the full-replacement publish mutation on the Centaur function contract: every call overwrites the snake's record wholesale, no delta or merge path exists, and the contract imposes no per-turn or per-second throttle — with tests for wholesale replacement, for a rapid intra-turn burst all being accepted, and for a direction dropped from a snapshot leaving no residue (`decision-transparency/snapshot-full-replacement#any-snapshot-stands-alone`, `#absence-is-meaningful`, `decision-transparency/hosting-server-sole-writer#cadence-is-the-writers-choice`)
- [ ] 2.4 Enforce sole writership at the function contract, not in the library: the caller must be the hosting server of the team owning the snake, and every other identity and surface — operator clients, another team's server, any programmatic surface — is rejected, with negative tests per rejected identity kind (`decision-transparency/hosting-server-sole-writer#only-the-hosting-server-writes`); the credential mechanics the check validates are `identity-and-authorization`'s, consumed here
- [ ] 2.5 Fix the slot discipline in the component's `AGENTS.md` and its exported surface: the recorded decision outputs are exactly two platform-wide slots — this per-snake record and the append-only action log (whose own contract is `replay-and-audit`'s to author) — with no per-team table or per-team column ever added, and a test or lint asserting no team-keyed schema extension exists (`decision-transparency/extensible-state-slots#novel-bot-same-schema`, `#no-per-team-slots`)
## 3. Publishing the record — Centaur Server (`packages/centaur-server-lib/`)

- [ ] 3.1 Implement the snapshot builder over the framework's evaluation state: for each direction with a defined stateMap entry, emit the score, the single world that produced it, and that same world's per-heuristic outputs each paired with the weight and label the composition used — one world per direction entry, with a test that the outputs decompose the emitted score and never come from a fresher world, that the weighted contributions sum to the emitted score, that a weight or nickname edited between composition and publication does not change what is emitted, that the world's per-snake turn timestamps are inside the emitted record so a renderer needs nothing else to mark frozen snakes, and that every recorded direction carries the same heuristics at the same weights — the writer drops none of what composition handed it, and composition answers every candidate with every heuristic (`bot-framework/total-heuristic-coverage#no-candidate-goes-unscored`), which is what makes a per-heuristic comparison across directions computable from exactly this set (`decision-transparency/computed-display-state#per-direction-coherence`, `#weights-and-labels-are-recorded-not-joined`, `#timestamps-travel-with-the-world`, `#the-same-heuristics-under-every-direction`)
- [ ] 3.2 Implement deterministic worst-case-world selection: among active worlds tying for a direction's minimum, pick by a fixed rule, with a test that the same evaluation state produces byte-identical snapshots across runs and across process restarts; carry the concrete rule as mechanism with a `// design:` reference to this change's archive folder (`decision-transparency/computed-display-state#worst-case-world-is-deterministic`)
- [ ] 3.3 Wire publication to the dirty flag: a full snapshot goes out whenever the flag is set, without waiting on a submission or turn event, and selection promotion of a manual-mode snake yields fresh snapshots so the operator's displays converge off the unselected tier — the flag and the promotion rescoring are `bot-framework`'s, consumed here (`decision-transparency/snapshot-full-replacement#dirty-flag-drives-publication`, `#selection-converges-the-display`)
- [ ] 3.4 Confine heuristic-contract-violation diagnosis to the hosting server's process log — no violation field on the record, no operator-interface surface, substituted values published like any other, with a test that a violating heuristic changes the published record only through its substituted value (`decision-transparency/published-slots-only#violations-stay-in-the-server-log`); the containment and substitution themselves are `bot-framework/author-fault-containment`
- [ ] 3.5 Pair every published snapshot with its snapshot-category action-log entry written in the same transaction as the record update, against the log contract `replay-and-audit` owns (`decision-transparency/hosting-server-sole-writer`, seam: `replay-and-audit/actors-write-own-entries#every-snapshot-logged`)

## 4. Team-private read scope — live vs post-game (Convex function contract)

- [ ] 4.1 Implement the read query and live subscription that serves a team its own snakes' display records, with read authorization enforced in the Centaur subsystem's own function contract as the single place — the displays of section 5 get no second, wider path (`decision-transparency/extensible-state-slots`, `decision-transparency/computed-display-state`)
- [ ] 4.2 Privacy enforcement as an explicit deliverable: negative tests that an opposing team's operator, an unauthenticated caller, and a spectator connection are all refused a team's recorded deliberation — heuristic weights, portfolio state, worst-case worlds, any display record — while the game is live, and that no display component can assemble another team's record from any surface it does hold (`decision-transparency/computed-display-state`, seam: `global-invariants/team-private-centaur-state#opponent-cannot-read-deliberation`, `live-game-observation/team-private-live-state#cross-team-reads-denied`)
- [ ] 4.3 Implement the coach/admin parity read path over the same query — a bound team's coaches and platform admins read exactly what a member reads and no more — as the read-scope grant `live-game-observation` defines, not a scope this capability invents
- [ ] 4.4 Resolve and implement the live-to-post-game visibility boundary — the highest-risk seam in this capability: decide with the owners whether a finished game's display records become readable to any authenticated user with the game record (feeding `replay-and-audit`'s team-perspective replay) or stay team-scoped like the team's configuration, then implement the transition and test it at both sides of the game-finished edge. The decision is now confined to the *record*: because scoring-time weights and labels are recorded, no replay path reads the team's live configuration, so whichever way this lands the boundary never has to cut through configuration (seam: `replay-and-audit/finished-games-public`, `replay-and-audit/team-perspective-replay`, `global-invariants/team-private-centaur-state#finished-games-release-only-what-is-published`; `decision-transparency/computed-display-state#weights-and-labels-are-recorded-not-joined`)

## 5. Operator decision displays (`apps/centaur-server-reference/`)

- [ ] 5.2 Implement the snapshot binding: a per-snake store that replaces its contents wholesale on every delivery — never merging, never back-filling a key absent from the newest snapshot — plus the shared absent-value rendering primitive every display uses in place of a value the record does not carry, and no heuristic evaluation or simulation code anywhere in the client bundle; tests for replacement, for withdrawal rendering absent, and for resuming from fresh state after subscription loss (`decision-transparency/snapshot-full-replacement#absence-is-meaningful`, `#any-snapshot-stands-alone`, `decision-transparency/published-slots-only#missing-cell-renders-absent`, `#no-client-recomputation`)
- [ ] 5.3 Implement the scored-direction display on both surfaces from one score and one monotone ramp — the four board cells adjacent to the examined snake's head, and the score labels on the direction affordance wherever that affordance is presented — with a distinct neutral state for a direction with no published score that is distinguishable from every point on the ramp, a test that the two surfaces never disagree, and a test that the cells still render for an examined snake whose staging affordance is absent (`decision-transparency/scored-direction-display#one-ramp-two-surfaces`, `#neutral-is-not-worst`, `#cells-need-no-holder`)
- [ ] 5.4 Guarantee the decoration never gates: with no display record for the held snake the staging affordance stays fully usable with its score decoration simply absent — test that a framework outage or an unpublished snake disables no human control (`decision-transparency/scored-direction-display#display-decorates-never-gates`)
- [ ] 5.5 Implement the worst-case world preview as a board overlay layer: current positions solid, the recorded world's simulated positions translucent, frozen snakes marked from the record's own per-snake turn timestamps; it appears the moment a direction becomes the examined one, re-renders in place on each new snapshot, and renders nothing at all with no examined direction or no record (`decision-transparency/worst-case-preview#pick-triggers-preview`, `#preview-evolves-in-place`, `#no-record-no-preview`)
- [ ] 5.6 Implement the per-direction decision breakdown table — one row per heuristic recorded in the examined direction's entry, showing that entry's own label, normalised output, weight, weighted contribution and relative impact — the last computed as that heuristic's weighted contribution for the examined direction minus the mean of its weighted contributions over every direction the same snapshot records (a signed centred difference, never a share of the score), reading only that one snapshot — reactive both to new snapshots and to a change of examined direction, with **no join against live configuration anywhere in the component**: tests that the contributions sum to the snapshot's recorded score, that a heuristic contributing identically to every recorded direction shows zero relative impact on all of them, that a snapshot carrying only some of the snake's directions centres on exactly those, and that rendering an older snapshot after a weight edit and a nickname change still shows the recorded values and still sums (`decision-transparency/decision-breakdown#rows-explain-the-recorded-world`, `#contributions-sum-to-the-recorded-score`, `#uniform-heuristic-has-zero-relative-impact`, `#the-mean-ranges-over-the-snapshots-candidates`, `#weight-edits-do-not-rewrite-the-past`, `#direction-switch-is-a-re-read`, `#snapshot-updates-the-open-table`)
- [ ] 5.7 Implement the examined selection as a client-local provider — at most one snake within the viewer's read scope and at most one of its candidate directions, held in client UI state alone, written to no store and no recorded slot, invisible to other clients, and independent of holding — with the three displays of 5.3/5.5/5.6 parameterised by (snake, direction, snapshot) and taking their subject from it. Tests: examining writes nothing and stages nothing; a snake nobody holds is examinable and renders every panel; the selection does not survive reload and never appears on another client (`decision-transparency/examined-subject#examining-is-not-acting`, `#any-readable-snake-is-examinable`, `#never-persisted`)
- [ ] 5.8 Wire the operator's one gesture: a pick on the four-direction staging affordance stages the direction *and* sets it as the examined direction, while every other route to examining a direction stages nothing — with a test that a coach-mode or replay-bound instance of the same components can change the examined direction with no mutation attempted at all (`decision-transparency/examined-subject#operator-pick-also-examines`, seam: `operator-control/board-and-move-interface#no-second-direction-selector`, `replay-and-audit/replay-binding-mutation-free`)
